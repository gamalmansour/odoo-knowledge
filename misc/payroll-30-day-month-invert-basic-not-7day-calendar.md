# Payroll on a 30-Day Month: Invert the BASIC Formula — Never Fake a 7-Day Calendar

## Problem

> Business requirement (common in Egypt/Gulf): the monthly salary is always based on a
> **30-day month** — a full month present = full wage. But a BASIC salary rule written as
> "sum the worked-days lines × wage/30" only sees work entries on calendar working days,
> so a real 5-day week yields ~22 days and the salary comes out short.
>
> The tempting "fix" is to edit the working schedule to 7 days/week so work entries cover
> the whole month. **This fixes payroll display and silently breaks everything else**:
> time off Thu→Sun counts 4 days instead of 2, attendance expects employees on weekends,
> overtime baselines inflate, and stray "weekly rest" work entries pile up.

The additive anti-pattern:

```python
# BASIC — WRONG: hostage to how many work entry days exist in the month
total_days = WORK100 + LEAVE120 + LEAVE130 + LEAVE140   # needs 30 lines to pay full wage
result = version.wage / 30 * min(total_days, 30)
```

## Root Cause

Work entries are generated **only on calendar attendance days** — that is by design and
is what makes leave counting, attendance and overtime correct. A salary formula that
*adds up* work entry days therefore couples the wage to the calendar shape. The coupling,
not the calendar, is the bug.

## Solution ✅

Keep the working schedule REAL (5 days, true hours) and make BASIC **subtractive**:
full wage minus wage/30 per unpaid day. Then the number of working days per month
(22, 23, February…) becomes irrelevant.

```python
# BASIC — RIGHT: full monthly wage, deduct only unpaid absence at wage/30 per day
unpaid_days = sum(wd.number_of_days for wd in payslip.worked_days_line_ids if not wd.is_paid)
result = version.wage - version.wage / 30 * min(unpaid_days, 30)
```

`wd.is_paid` is driven by the structure's **Unpaid Work Entry Types**
(`hr.payroll.structure.unpaid_work_entry_type_ids`) — make sure the unpaid leave type
(e.g. `LEAVE90`) is listed there.

Clean-up checklist after undoing a 7-day-calendar hack:
1. Remove weekend rows from `resource.calendar.attendance` (see
   [timeoff-counts-weekend-check-resource-calendar.md](timeoff-counts-weekend-check-resource-calendar.md)).
2. Fix `hour_from`/`hour_to` and `hours_per_day` if they were inflated to fake hours.
3. Regenerate work entries with the standard wizard
   (`hr.work.entry.regeneration.wizard` — it skips validated entries).
4. Purge stale weekend entries the wizard can't reach: draft/conflict entries on weekend
   days with `leave_id = False` (they were only ever generated by the hacked calendar).
5. Verify with a throwaway payslip inside `env.cr.savepoint()`: full month → BASIC ==
   wage exactly; one unpaid day → wage − wage/30.

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- **Sandwich policy is a business decision**: unpaid leave Thu→Sun deducts 2 days (work
  days) or 4 (weekend inside counts)? Ask HR before writing the formula; the subtractive
  formula above implements the 2-day policy since work entries only exist on work days.
- Any OTHER rule that sums day counts (per-day allowances, meal/transport) has the same
  coupling — audit the whole structure, not just BASIC.
- Old validated payslips keep their historical lines; the fix affects future computations.

## Versions

Implemented and verified on Odoo 19 Enterprise (`hr_payroll` + `l10n_eg_hr_payroll` installed).
